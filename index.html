About
Systems engineer · Militia Capital (long/short equity fund)

The Short Version

At Militia Capital I ship the automation backbone of the firm: the LP portal investors log into, the screener the desk shorts from, the engine that builds our regulatory filings, the monitors that watch the SEC so nobody has to, and the marketing materials that rebuild themselves from live fund data every month.

Every one of those started as hours of recurring human work. Now they're systems — scheduled, audited, and running in production. That's the job I do: find the loop a human is stuck in, and delete it.

Why It Works

  • I ship complete systems solo — ingest, scoring, UI, deploy, docs — at a pace teams don't expect from one person.
  • Spec-first: PRDs, phased plans, and coverage matrices before code.
  • I audit like a regulator: ground-truth reconciliation, changelogs, head-to-head feed verification.
  • I cost-engineer LLM stacks — same output quality at a fraction of the spend.
  • Fluent in regulated contexts: SEC filings, marketing review, records retention, investor onboarding.
Process
The same discipline on every build — visible in the commit history.
